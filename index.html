💡 IT Security Tips of the Week
📱

Never share MFA codes over the phone

No IT staff or Microsoft employee will ever ask for your 6-digit code. Hang up and call IT directly.

🔒

Use a unique password for every account

Password reuse is one of the most common ways accounts get compromised. Use the firm-approved password manager.

✉

Verify unexpected requests via a second channel

Urgent email asking for a wire or file? Confirm via Teams or a direct phone call before acting.

↻

Keep Windows and Office updated

Most cyberattacks exploit known patched vulnerabilities. Never dismiss update notifications.

📶

Always use the firm VPN on public Wi-Fi

Always connect via the firm VPN when working from a coffee shop, hotel, or airport.

👁

Lock your screen when stepping away

Press Win+L (Windows) or Cmd+Ctrl+Q (Mac). An unlocked screen is an open door to client data.
